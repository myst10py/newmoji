Congress passes $1.2 trillion spending bill

💡 First time here?
Tap below to reveal more info step-by-step

What Happened

Congress passed a $1.2 trillion spending bill to keep the government funded through September 2024. The bill includes $60 billion for disaster relief and $20 billion for infrastructure projects, but cuts $15 billion from climate programs.

The vote was 314-108 in the House and 68-29 in the Senate, with bipartisan support.
